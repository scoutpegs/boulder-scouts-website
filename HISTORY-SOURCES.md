# Boulder Scout Group history — source notes

This file records what public sources support and what remains a group-supplied historical account. Do not remove the labels on `about.html` unless an original record supports the relevant detail.

## Public/official sources used

- **Kalgoorlie Miner, 10 December 2018 — “Historian and food charity recognised”**: the volunteer-awards list names Brenda Clark of 1st Boulder Scout Group as the individual Youth category award recipient. This supports the published recognition milestone, but does not by itself verify the separate group-supplied council dispute story in 2021. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/historian-and-food-charity-recognised-ng-b881045019z
- **Kalgoorlie Miner, 2019 — “Boulder cub scouts tidy up Kalgoorlie arboretum”**: published report about Boulder Cub Scouts taking part in an Arboretum clean-up. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/boulder-cub-scouts-tidy-up-kalgoorlie-arboretum-ng-b881403679z
- **Kalgoorlie Miner, 22 May 2020 — “Boulder Scout Group stays prepared with Zoom catch-up meetings during pandemic”**: published report documenting Zoom catch-ups during pandemic restrictions. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/boulder-scout-group-stays-prepared-with-zoom-catch-up-meetings-during-pandemic-ng-b881540623z
- **Kalgoorlie Miner — “Scouts group questions decision to hand old building to social society”**: published report about the group questioning a decision concerning a nearby community building. The supplied story relates this to late 2021; the page is linked without asserting an exact meeting date. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/scouts-group-questions-decision-to-hand-old-building-to-social-society-c-4970725
- **Kalgoorlie Miner, 1 September 2022 — “Rotary Club of Boulder donates funds to continue Boulder Scouts’ traditions”**: article states Rotary donated $2,500 toward equipment/signage and $1,000 towards hall restumping support, and notes the upgraded section flags. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/rotary-club-of-boulder-donates-funds-to-continue-boulder-scouts-traditions-c-8040275
- **Kalgoorlie Miner — “Boulder Scout Group reaches $100,000 fundraising goal to restump ageing hall”**: published report supporting the $100,000 fundraising milestone. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/boulder-scout-group-reaches-100000-fundraising-goal-to-restump-ageing-hall-c-12480948
- **Kalgoorlie Miner — “Boulder Scout Group celebrates much-anticipated reopening of historic hall”**: published report about the restored hall's reopening. https://www.kalminer.com.au/news/kalgoorlie-miner/boulder-scout-group-celebrates-much-anticipated-reopening-of-historic-hall-c-15108907
- **Scouts Australia, 26 February 2025 — “AJ2025: What a quest!”**: official general details about the 26th Australian Scout Jamboree. It does not independently verify the Boulder contingent's size or fundraising details. https://scouts.com.au/blog/2025/02/26/aj2025-what-a-quest/
- **Scouts Australia, 28 July 2026 — “Adult Recognition Awards 2026”**: listing identifies Shane Treen as Assistant Scout Leader, Boulder Scout Group. The listing consulted does not state the award level beside his name. https://scouts.com.au/blog/2026/07/28/scouts-australia-adult-recognition-awards-2026/
- **National Redress Scheme institution listing — Boulder Scout Group**: lists the group at 10 Hamilton Street, Boulder, and notes Scout Groups started in Australia in 1908. This does not prove that Boulder itself began in 1908. https://www.nationalredress.gov.au/institutions/boulder-scout-group
- **Trove archival lead (provided)**: https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/article/32517097. The page could not be directly inspected in this research pass; treat it as a lead for further archive verification, not proof of the site’s 1908/1944/1946 assertions.

## Group-supplied account needing original corroboration

- Boulder Scout Group was established specifically in **1908**.
- The troop stood guard at the **November 1944** funeral of Sophia Mabel Hopkins at the request of the Acting District Commissioner.
- A former **WWII aircraft hangar was cut in half and transported in 1946**, with one half going to Campbell Street and the other becoming the Boulder hall on Hamilton Street.
- The exact **2024 Kalgoorlie relaunch date**, three-year hiatus, and Boulder membership exceeding 90 at that time.
- The exact hall restoration counts of **108 replacement stumps and six beams**, “Sponsor-a-Stump” campaign details and the exact reopening date of **23 June 2024**.
- The exact Boulder contingent size of **10 youth and 2 leaders** at AJ2025 and the group fundraising narrative.

Suggested evidence to locate: district/region minutes, Scout council records, newspaper scans, council building/works files, hall committee financial records, original photos with dates, AJ2025 group reports, or family documents. Please obtain permission before publishing identifiable information about children or living members.

## Further local verification resource

- **City of Kalgoorlie-Boulder Local History Archives**: the City says the archive holds council documents, historical books and Goldfields newspapers on microfilm dating from 1895. It is a useful research destination for the group-supplied 1908 founding, 1944 funeral guard, 1946 hangar transfer and hall works records; it does not itself establish those details. https://www.ckb.wa.gov.au/our-community/arts-culture-education/heritage-services-tours/local-history-archives.aspx
