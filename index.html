Scouts in Boulder, Western Australia

Boulder Scout Group is a local youth Scouting group based at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432. We welcome enquiries from families in Boulder and Kalgoorlie-Boulder and list four sections: Joey Scouts (5–8), Cub Scouts (8–11), Scouts (11–14) and Venturer Scouts (15–18). Young people build outdoor skills, confidence and friendships through camping, hiking, practical challenges, teamwork and community projects.

New families can try three weeks free after contacting the group. We will help you find the right section and confirm the next meeting before you visit. No uniform or previous Scouting experience is needed for a first visit.

Our calendar lists confirmed Boulder Scout Group meetings and activities when they are published. For current Scouts WA events across the region, use the official event listing; a date or activity is only confirmed when it appears in the current source.

Contact: gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au. Meeting dates and locations are taken from the group calendar; do not assume a regular night for a section unless it is published.
