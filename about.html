About Boulder Scout Group

Boulder Scout Group is based at Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432. This site provides information about the group’s four youth sections, its public calendar, how to arrange a first visit and official Scouting resources.

The history page labels the evidence behind each milestone. Some early dates and details are group-supplied accounts awaiting confirmation from original records; they should not be presented as independently verified facts.

Contact the group at gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au.
