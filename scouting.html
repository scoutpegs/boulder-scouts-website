What is Scouting?

Scouting is a worldwide youth movement. In Australia it is run by Scouts Australia, and Boulder Scout Group is one local group that belongs to it.

The Promise

Every member, from a Joey to a leader, makes the same Australian Scout Promise. In short: do your best, stay true to your spiritual beliefs, contribute to your community and the world, help other people, and live by the Scout Law. There are two official wordings, and each Scout picks the one that suits them. The full text is on the Scouts Australia Promise and Law page.

The Law

One Law covers every section:

  • Be respectful. Treat people, places and the environment with care, and be friendly and considerate.
  • Do what is right. Be honest, fair and reliable, and own up when you get it wrong.
  • Believe in myself. Have a go, keep learning and do your best, whatever the result.

How it works: the Scout Method

The Promise and Law are one part of the Scout Method, the way every section runs its program. Young people learn by doing, in small teams, outdoors where possible, with adult volunteers backing them up and the youth members helping decide what happens next. That is why a Cub night looks different to a Venturer night even though the same ideas sit underneath.

Four Areas of Personal Growth

Activities in every section are planned to give a mix of these four.

Outdoors

Hiking, paddling, camping and expeditions.

Creative

Making, designing and performing.

Community

Service projects that make a difference.

Personal Growth

Challenges that build confidence.

Where our sections fit

Scouting in Australia runs from Joey Scouts through to Rovers. This group lists four sections: Joey Scouts (5 to 8), Cub Scouts (8 to 11), Scouts (11 to 14) and Venturer Scouts (15 to 18). Young people move up together as they get older and keep the badges they have earned.
