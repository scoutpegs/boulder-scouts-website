Scouts in Boulder

Boulder Scout Group’s website lists Scouts for ages 11–14. The group highlights outdoor skills, camping, hiking, navigation, patrol teamwork, community projects and practical challenges.

Contact gl.boulder@scoutswa.com.au before the first visit to confirm the right meeting night and details. Families can try three weeks free.

Hall: Boulder Scout Hall, 10 Hamilton Street South, Boulder WA 6432, Australia. Dates and event locations are shown on the confirmed group calendar.
